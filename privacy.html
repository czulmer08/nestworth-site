Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

NestWorth is a personal budgeting tool. It is built so that your financial information stays in your own Google account and is never sent to, seen by, or stored by us. This policy explains exactly what that means.

The short version: your budget lives in a private spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. NestWorth runs entirely in your browser. We — the developer — do not collect, receive, store, or have access to your budget, your transactions, or your financial data. There are no ads, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

Who we are

NestWorth is provided by Ulmer Consulting, LLC ("we," "us"). You can reach us at czulmer@gmail.com.

What information NestWorth uses

  • Your budget data (categories, transactions, goals, net-worth figures) is stored only in a Google Sheets file that NestWorth creates in your own Google Drive. It never passes through any server of ours — the app reads and writes that file directly from your browser to your Google account.
  • Your Google account email is read while you are signed in, only to show you which account is active and to create your budget file. We do not store it.
  • Sign-in permission. NestWorth requests a single, limited Google permission (drive.file). This lets it see and manage only the files it creates or that you specifically open with it — it cannot see the rest of your Google Drive, your email, your contacts, or anything else in your account.
  • On-device settings (such as your theme choice) are saved only in your own browser's local storage on your device. They are not transmitted to us. (The optional receipt-scanning key is handled as described below.)

Optional receipt & statement scanning

Scanning is off unless you turn it on. If you choose to enable it, you add your own free Google "Gemini" API key. The key is stored on your device and, so it survives your device clearing its storage, saved with your budget in your own private Google Drive — it is never sent to us. (On a budget you have shared with others, people with access to that budget could use the key.) When you scan a receipt or statement, that image is sent from your device to Google's Gemini API to read the text, and the result is placed into your budget for you to review. We never receive your key or your images. Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's own terms. You can remove the key at any time in Settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or store your financial data on any server.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party tracking or analytics.
  • We do not access any files in your Drive other than the budget file(s) you use with NestWorth.

Sharing a budget with family

If you choose to share a budget, you do so through Google Drive's own sharing — you decide who to invite, and you can change or remove access at any time from Google Drive. People you invite can see and edit that shared budget; each person's own separate budget stays private to them.

Google API Services — Limited Use

NestWorth's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide and improve the features you see in the app, is never transferred to us or to others except as needed to provide those features, is never used for advertising, and is never sold.

Keeping your data & deleting it

Because your data lives in your own Google Drive, you are always in control of it. To remove your NestWorth data, delete the budget spreadsheet from your Google Drive. To revoke NestWorth's access entirely, remove it under your Google Account ▸ Data & privacy ▸ Third-party access.

Children

NestWorth is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of NestWorth after a change means you accept the updated policy.
