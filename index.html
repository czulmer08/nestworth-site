What it is

Money planning that understands real life.

Your spending, your paychecks, your savings goals, your cash flow and your net worth — working together in one private place, instead of scattered across five different apps. Log what you spend in a couple of taps, plan around your actual paydays, save toward what matters, and see where your year is headed. No spreadsheets to wrestle, nothing to download — NestWorth builds you a private space and quietly does the math for you.

What makes it different

Built for real life.

Your paycheck isn't always monthly.

NestWorth understands weekly and biweekly pay, the three-paycheck months, raises mid-year, and the money you set aside from each check before it ever reaches your spending budget.

Saving shouldn't disappear from your net worth.

Money you move toward a goal is treated as savings — not spending — and it stays properly reflected in your bigger financial picture. Putting $800 into a car fund doesn't look like $800 gone.

Your budget remembers.

Unused money in a category can carry forward, and a shared contingency cushion absorbs the months when real life doesn't follow the plan — so one tight week doesn't quietly break everything else.

See a big decision before you make it.

Wondering what buying the car — or taking the trip — would do to the rest of your year? NestWorth can model the choice against your real plan, not just tell you what you spent last month.

Meet Wren

Your money, explained in plain English.

Ask what's driving your spending this month, whether you're on pace for a goal, why things feel tighter than usual, or what a big purchase would mean for your plan. Wren answers from the very same numbers NestWorth uses everywhere else — it never makes up its own.

NestWorth computes. Wren interprets.

The math matters here

Built carefully. Tested relentlessly.

This isn't a play app. Your budget, your cash flow, your savings goals and your net worth should never tell four different stories — so NestWorth runs on one financial model across the whole app, and that model is checked against many thousands of real-life situations: refunds, irregular paydays, three-paycheck months, money moved into goals, future-dated transactions, and the messy edge cases real households actually run into. When the numbers show up, they agree. See how NestWorth works & how we test it →

Your financial life doesn't live on our servers.

NestWorth creates your budget inside your own Google Drive and connects to it straight from your browser, using a single limited Google permission. There's no NestWorth database holding your household's numbers, and no advertising profile built on your spending. Only you can see your budget — not the person who shared the app, not anyone else. Getting started

Three taps and you're in.

  1. Tap “Open NestWorth” above. — or open the link someone sent you, then sign in with Google and approve the one permission NestWorth asks for.
  2. NestWorth builds your budget. It sets up a private budget just for you automatically, then a quick setup helps you add your spending categories and income. Takes a few seconds — and you can change anything later.
  3. Add it to your home screen. On iPhone (Safari): the Share button ▸ Add to Home Screen ▸ Add. On Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ ▸ Add to Home screen. Now it opens in one tap, like a real app.

Personal and family? Once you’re in, open Settings ▸ Your budgets ▸ New budget to create a shared household budget, then use Settings ▸ Share this budget to invite your family. A name pill appears at the top to switch between them — and everyone still keeps their own private one.

Want to snap photos of receipts? It's optional — in the app, open Settings ▸ Receipt scanning and paste a free key. Skip it if you'd rather just type.
