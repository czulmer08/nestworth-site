What it is

A simple money tracker that lives on your phone.

Log what you spend in a couple of taps, see where it goes, set budgets you can actually keep, and watch your net worth grow over time — no spreadsheets to wrestle, nothing to download. NestWorth builds you a private space and does the math for you.

Getting started

Three taps and you're in.

1. Tap “Open NestWorth” above. Sign in with your Google account if it asks.

2. Tap “Create my budget.” NestWorth sets up a private budget just for you.

3. Add it to your home screen. On iPhone use Safari’s Share button → Add to Home Screen. On Android use Chrome’s menu → Add to Home screen.

Your numbers stay yours.
NestWorth creates your budget inside your own Google Drive, where only you can see it. Everyone who uses NestWorth gets their own private space.

Personal and family? Once you’re in, tap your budget’s name at the top of the app to add a shared household budget and switch between them — everyone still keeps their own private one.

Want to snap photos of receipts? It’s optional — in the app, open Settings → Receipt scanning and paste a free key.
