What it is

A simple money tracker that lives on your phone.

Log what you spend in a couple of taps, see where it goes, set budgets you can actually keep, and watch your net worth grow over time — no spreadsheets to wrestle, nothing to download. NestWorth builds you a private space and does the math for you.

Getting started

Three taps and you're in.

  1. Tap “Open NestWorth” above. — or open the link someone sent you. Sign in with your Google account if it asks.
  2. Tap “Create my budget.” NestWorth sets up a private budget just for you and approves the one permission it needs. Takes a few seconds.
  3. Add it to your home screen. On iPhone (Safari): the Share button ▸ Add to Home Screen ▸ Add. On Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ ▸ Add to Home screen. Now it opens in one tap, like a real app.

Your numbers stay yours.

NestWorth creates your budget inside your own Google Drive, where only you can see it. Not the person who shared the app, not anyone else. Everyone who uses NestWorth gets their own private space — nothing is shared or pooled.

Personal and family? Once you’re in, open Settings ▸ Your budgets ▸ + New budget to create a shared household budget, then tap Share with others to invite your family. A name pill appears at the top to switch between them — and everyone still keeps their own private one.

Want to snap photos of receipts? It's optional — in the app, open Settings ▸ Receipt scanning and paste a free key. Skip it if you'd rather just type.
