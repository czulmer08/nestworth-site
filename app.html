    Already tracked spending elsewhere? Bring it in so your budget suggestions and history are right from day one. Pick how you'd like to do it:
    Everything is copied into this budget's spreadsheet. It's a one-time copy — the app can't stay linked to your bank or to apps like Empower or Mint. Only money you spent is brought in (payments and refunds are skipped).
    The friendly way, especially on a phone — enter your rows in a spreadsheet, then read them in here.
    1. Tap Open the Import tab — it's added to your budget sheet with the right columns.
    2. Type or paste your transactions there: Date, Category, Amount (Company, Description, Payee optional).
    3. Come back and tap Read them in. Your rows are cleared from the Import tab afterward.
    Paste rows from a spreadsheet or an export. Put columns in this order: Date, Category, Amount (then optional Company, Description). A header row is fine.
