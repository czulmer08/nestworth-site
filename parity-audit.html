If you build in one order, this one

The genuinely impactful user-facing gaps, roughly highest-value first. Everything else below is either already there or a smaller refinement.

  1. 1Multi-year budgets. The new app is locked to the current calendar year — no way to view or build a past or future year. The old app had a year selector and "+ Add year."
  2. 2Insights depth. The old Insights had a month-by-month spend chart, a this-year / last-12-months toggle, year-over-year movers, a "trending up" read, per-category trend sparklines, and a full-year projection. The new one shows only this-month-vs-last-month + top 3.
  3. 3Search filters. New search is text-only. The old one filtered by category, month, and amount range, with scope (this year / all time) and sort.
  4. 4Net-worth reconcile step. After importing balances, the old app prompted you to earmark set-aside goals into their holding account so money isn't double-counted. Now you must remember to do it in each goal.
  5. 5Backdated deposits & prior-year entries. Logging into a past month shows no deposit target/progress, and expense feedback only works within the current year.
  6. 6Category-linked goals that truly integrate. A goal tied to a category is just a tag now — it doesn't raise that category's budget or show nested under its card the way the old app did.
  7. 7Smaller wins: wipe/blank data, hide-payee/reimbursed form prefs, merge several categories into one at once, a one-tap "budget from last year," link a budget by URL, and remove a budget from the list.

Deliberately not carried over

These depended on Google Apps Script running on a server or writing formulas into the sheet. The pure client-side rewrite handles them a different way, or can't.

  • Emailed reminders — no server to send mail. Replaced by the in-app reminder card on the Month tab (same five nudges), but nothing arrives when the app is closed, and there's no recipient list or schedule.
  • Two-way sheet sync & the checkbox-to-import trigger — relied on Apps Script onEdit. The app now reads the Import tab on a button tap and manages itemized bills in-app instead.
  • "Setup & Help" tab and on-sheet itemized detail rows — the app is the interface now; bills live in its hidden meta sheet rather than as formula rows on the summary tab.
